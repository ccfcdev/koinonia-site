register.

The things people ask the office most about Koinonia.

Who can come to Koinonia?

Everyone is welcome: members, friends and first-time guests from every CCFC church in Zambia, Zimbabwe, Malawi and the wider family.

When and where is Koi 26'?

Koi 26' runs from Friday to Sunday, 18 to 20 December 2026, at Grace Exploits Event Center, Don Gordon Road (opposite Don Gordon School), Chainama Minestone, Lusaka. Its theme is The RESET, from Psalm 51:10-12, Isaiah 43:18-19 and 2 Corinthians 5:17.

How much does it cost?

Delegates from Zambia: K500 for adults (13 and over) and K250 for children (12 and under). International delegates: USD 25 for adults and USD 10 for children. Registration is confirmed once the fee is paid.

I am travelling from Zimbabwe, Malawi or another country. Which fee do I pay?

The international fee: USD 25 for adults and USD 10 for children. International delegates are those travelling from outside Zambia. Delegates travelling from anywhere within Zambia, Lusaka or out of town, pay the Zambia fee.

Can I register my children?

Yes. Koinonia is a family conference. Fill in the form for each child and choose their age range.

Can I come for just one or two days?

Yes. The registration form asks which days you will attend: Day 1, Day 2, Day 3 or all three. Register once and choose the days you can make.

Can I take part by preaching, singing or performing?

Yes. Choose Yes on the form and tell us what you would like to share. Slots are limited and the organisers will confirm.

How do I know my registration went through?

You get a registration number on the page straight away, and a confirmation email with the delegate fee that applies to you. The email confirms your registration, not payment.

What does Koinonia mean?

Koinonia is the New Testament Greek word for fellowship: sharing life together, not just a room. "They devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and to fellowship" (Acts 2:42). Once a year the churches of the CCFC family gather in Lusaka to remember that they are one body.

Who do I ask if I have a question?

WhatsApp the church office on +260 573 762 913, call +260 772 890 854, or email info@ccfczambia.org.
